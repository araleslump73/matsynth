How to use
  • RECORD (First Time): Press to arm recording. Play your first note to start capturing the base loop.
  • OVERDUB (Continuous Loop): Once a loop exists, press OVERDUB to add layers. The previous loop plays automatically, and you can record multiple passes. The countdown resets on each loop!
  • First Note Trigger: The first recording begins automatically when you play the first note.
  • Stop Recording: Press STOP when you're done overdubbing. The loop continues playing for the next layer.
  • PLAY: Manually plays back all recorded layers (not needed during overdub, it's automatic!).
  • CLEAR: Deletes all layers to start fresh from scratch.
  • Channel Selection: Choose which MIDI channel to record (e.g., Channel 10 for drums, Channel 1 for bass).
